Community Guidelines

CivicFix is built on trust, responsibility, and collective action. These guidelines help keep the platform useful, safe, and respectful for everyone.

🤝 Our Core Principles

  • Truth and transparency
  • Respect for people and public spaces
  • Responsible civic participation
  • Community-driven prioritization

✅ What You Should Do

Report Genuine Issues

Only report real civic problems you have personally observed. Provide accurate details and upload relevant images where possible.

Vote Responsibly

Use votes to support issues that genuinely affect the community. Voting helps prioritize what needs attention first.

Be Respectful

Treat fellow citizens, authorities, and communities with respect. Disagreements should focus on issues, not individuals.

❌ What Is Not Allowed

  • Fake, misleading, or duplicate reports
  • Abusive, hateful, or threatening language
  • Political campaigning or propaganda
  • Sharing personal or private information
  • Misuse of export or authority features

🔍 Verification & Moderation

Issues may be reviewed by the community or moderators. Activity patterns are monitored to prevent misuse. Verified issues are highlighted to maintain transparency.

🛡️ Account Responsibility

You are responsible for all activity performed using your account. Please keep your login credentials secure and report any suspicious behavior.

🌍 Our Commitment

CivicFix is committed to transparency, fairness, privacy protection, and empowering citizens to raise real issues that matter.

By using CivicFix, you agree to follow these community guidelines. Let’s work together to create meaningful civic impact.
